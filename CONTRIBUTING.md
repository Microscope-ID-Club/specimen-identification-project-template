# Contributing

Follow the project's scope, permissions, and data-governance decisions in `PROJECT.md` when adding or revising records.

Proposals concerning the general examination method, managed section of `AGENTS.md`, record format, or reusable template should normally be made in the relevant upstream repository:

- [Conversational specimen identification](https://github.com/Microscope-ID-Club/conversational-specimen-identification) for the method and record format;
- [Specimen-identification project template](https://github.com/Microscope-ID-Club/specimen-identification-project-template) for reusable project setup.

Project-specific instructions and records may be changed here in accordance with the project's own review process. Do not remove the managed marker comments from `AGENTS.md`, silently overwrite another contributor's observations, or add information that falls outside the permissions recorded in `PROJECT.md`.
